Today at a Glance

Today’s market is a tale of two AI giants: Microsoft (MSFT) soared over 15% after its cloud-fueled results, while Meta Platforms (META) sank 9% as investors recoiled from its heavy AI spending without a clear payoff. The Fed’s decision to hold rates steady gave the broader market a lift, helping chip stocks and the S&P 500 recover more than half of the prior day’s losses. Electrovaya (EFL) timed its launch of the ElvaPulse 1500 high-power energy storage platform perfectly for the data-center boom, and Coastal Financial (CCB) offered upbeat Q2 guidance that underscores regional bank resilience. With Treasury Sukuk auctions pulling in AED 4.83 billion and geopolitical tensions simmering around Iran, liquidity remains ample but fragile. Watch for this AI bifurcation to deepen as more earnings roll in—companies that monetize AI will run, while those that merely spend will get punished.
