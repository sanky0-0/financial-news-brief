Today at a Glance

Today’s most striking move comes from SLB (NYSE:SLB), surging on strong Q3 results and an unusually bullish 2027 outlook that signals a genuine Middle East rebound. That optimism contrasts with the broader energy map being redrawn by conflict: Qatar’s deal to buy U.S. natural gas shows how Iran tensions are permanently rerouting global LNG flows—watch for more long-term supply contracts shifting toward American exporters. In fixed income, battered bonds are finally attracting bargain hunters, hinting that the painful rate cycle may be opening a window for duration plays. Meanwhile, Google CEO Sundar Pichai’s latest comments on AI self-improvement underscore that the tech disruption story is far from priced in. Today matters because it offers a rare convergence of a cyclical energy turnaround, a potential bond-market pivot, and a reminder that AI’s next leap is already being sketched in boardrooms.
