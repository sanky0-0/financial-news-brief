Why this matters: - Mandatory Aadhaar authentication for subsidized LPG refills from Oct 1 directly impacts India’s Rs 6,100 crore ($730M) FY2024 LPG subsidy program, targeting leakage among the 300M+ connections served by state-run oil marketing companies Indian Oil (IOC.NS), BPCL (BPCL.NS), and HPCL (HPCL.NS). BPCL.NS and HPCL.NS face similar balance-sheet relief. - The rule reinforces India’s targeted subsidy delivery, accelerating the shift to clean cooking fuels and potentially dampening kerosene demand.
