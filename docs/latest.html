Today at a Glance

South Korea’s Finance Minister Choi Sang-mok made the day’s boldest call, forecasting the semiconductor boom will run until 2027—a signal to keep riding AI-fueled chip demand through names like Samsung Electronics (005930.KS) and SK Hynix (000660.KS). Meanwhile, Canada’s fresh bet on a new oil pipeline offers a different kind of long-cycle promise: higher export volumes for producers such as Suncor (SU) and Cenovus (CVE), but no return of the old jobs boom, leaving investors to weigh pure output growth against political and labor crosswinds. In a specific corporate move, Equipment House (9518.SR) set a November 11 shareholder vote to approve a 3-million-share buyback, a near-term catalyst that could put a floor under the stock. Today matters because it juxtaposes secular tech optimism with an energy gambit that is about volumes, not employment, while handing traders a discrete event to price into a Saudi small cap.
