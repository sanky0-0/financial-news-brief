Today at a Glance

Today’s most interesting flashpoint is Australia’s gas-reservation debate, where critics argue the draft rules contain “Santos-sized loopholes” that would let Santos (ASX: STO) and Woodside (ASX: WDS) largely sidestep domestic supply obligations — so the precise wording from Resources Minister Madeleine King is the first thing to watch. Zelensky’s statement that Ukraine supports the ceasefire but Russia must also support it keeps the geopolitical risk premium alive; if the Kremlin demurs, expect European gas and defence stocks to stay bid, while a genuine truce would likely fade that premium. The “Past and Present” lens is useful because this is the same tension between export revenue and domestic energy security that has dogged Australia for years, and today’s reaction in STO, WDS, and the AUD will tell you whether investors believe the policy has teeth. For the future, watch whether Russia’s next move lowers LNG and oil prices, and whether election-year politics forces Canberra to close the loopholes — a result that would be bearish for producers and a relief for domestic gas buyers. Today matters because policy details, not headlines, will determine whether energy equities rally on a weak reservation regime or sell off on real supply constraints.
