## Today at a Glance
Despite futures pointing to a higher open, it’s oil and bonds that are setting the real tone today, as the insurance bill from the Iran conflict extends well beyond tankers and threatens to push global shipping and energy costs higher. On the macro front, the US Manufacturing PMI slipped in August, confirming that demand is softening and raising the stakes for Friday’s payrolls. A very different kind of momentum is building in India, where tighter FSSAI and FDA food-safety checks are opening a ₹1,200-crore market for AI-driven fraud detection in FMCG supply chains—a niche that could reward tech providers for the rest of the year. And in a rare ground-level signal, a Belarusian combine operator disclosed earning 13,000 BYN in 40 days, offering a glimpse of rural wage pressure inside an economy strained by sanctions. Today matters because the collision of slowing growth and an expanding war-risk premium could force the Fed’s hand on rate cuts, while the FMCG fraud-detection surge tells you exactly where regulatory budgets are heading.
