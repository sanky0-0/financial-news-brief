# Daily Financial Brief

## Today at a Glance
Today’s market is flashing a rotation signal: BofA warns that the era of “easy profits” from the AI boom is over, with weakening white-collar spending dimming the sector’s luster, just as traditional finance steps back into the spotlight. Morgan Stanley upgraded Wells Fargo (WFC) to Overweight, citing a stronger balance sheet and improving earnings outlook, a vote of confidence in old-economy banking. Meanwhile, a record flood of cash into municipal and Treasury bond funds—sending the iShares 20+ Year Treasury Bond ETF (TLT) higher—underscores a broader flight to safety. In a longer-term infrastructure play, SpaceX proposed a new Florida natural gas pipeline to fuel its Cape Canaveral launches, signaling sustained private investment in space logistics. The day matters because it crystallizes the shift from speculative tech toward value, income, and tangible assets.
