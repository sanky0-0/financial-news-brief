### Semiconductors
**Semiconductor market to remain solid ‘at least through next year,’ South Korea finance minister says** — [→ cnbc.com](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/17/south-korea-semiconductor-outlook-lee-hyoung-il.html)

**Why this matters:**
- South Korea’s finance minister sees the semiconductor market staying solid through 2025, directly benefiting memory giants **Samsung Electronics (005930.KS)** and **SK Hynix (000660.KS)** — together controlling ~70% of the global DRAM and ~50% of NAND flash supply.
- The upbeat outlook suggests sustained capex cycles for these chipmakers, supporting equipment and materials suppliers like **ASML (ASML)** and **Applied Materials (AMAT)**, which count Samsung and SK Hynix as top customers.
- With AI-driven demand for high-bandwidth memory (HBM) remaining a key growth driver, the statement reinforces confidence in SK Hynix — NVIDIA’s primary HBM3E supplier — and Samsung’s push to qualify its own HBM for NVIDIA’s Blackwell platform.
