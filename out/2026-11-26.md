**SAP Weighs Cutting at Least 10,000 Jobs in Its AI Overhaul** — [→ trendingtopics.at](https://www.trendingtopics.eu/sap-job-cuts-ai-investments/)

**Why this matters:**
- SAP’s plan to cut 10,000+ jobs (~9% of its 108,000 workforce) to fund an AI overhaul sets a precedent for enterprise software peers — watch for similar restructuring announcements from Oracle (ORCL) and Salesforce (CRM), which are also racing to embed AI across their platforms.
- The move could lift SAP’s (SAP) operating margins by 200–300 bps by 2025, but execution risk is high; any missteps in the Q2 earnings call could erase the stock’s 40% YTD gains.
- This AI-driven culling highlights a capital reallocation wave: spending on AI infrastructure (benefiting NVIDIA (NVDA), Microsoft (MSFT)) is surging while traditional IT services headcount (Accenture (ACN), Infosys (INFY)) faces structural pressure.
- With tech layoffs already topping 50,000 in 2024, SAP’s move signals that even profitable incumbents are prioritizing AI efficiency — a trend that could accelerate if rate cuts are delayed, squeezing valuations across the sector (XLK).
