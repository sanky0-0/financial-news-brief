**Why this matters:**
- Brent at $125–130/bbl is ~25% above Q1 averages, directly lifting upstream EPS for supermajors; ExxonMobil (XOM) and Chevron (CVX) could see Q2 earnings per share add $0.50–$0.70 just from price tailwinds — watch for guidance bumps in July updates.
