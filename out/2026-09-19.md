**Why this matters:**
- South Africa’s 700 kg plastic-to-asphalt trial signals scalable recycled polymer use in high-durability applications — a model that could slash substrate costs for semiconductor packaging firms like Amkor (AMKR) and ASE Technology (ASX) if adapted to chip encapsulation, where virgin epoxy resins dominate.
- This practical recycling leap pressures polymer suppliers to semiconductor fabs — such as Sumitomo Bakelite (4206.T) and Hitachi Chemical (now Showa Denko, 4004.T) — to accelerate bio-based or recycled-content molding compounds, especially as EU regulations tighten on single-use plastics in electronics.

### Energy
**LPG Aadhaar rule from October 1: Authentication mandatory for subsidised gas refills; how to complete process, check steps** — [→ timesofindia.indiatimes.com](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/business/india-business/lpg-aadhaar-rule-from-october-1-authentication-mandatory-for-subsidised-gas-refills-how-to-complete-process-check-steps/articleshow/134356757.cms)

**Why this matters:**
- Mandatory Aadhaar authentication for subsidized LPG refills from Oct 1 directly impacts India’s Rs 6,100 crore ($730M) FY2024 LPG subsidy program, targeting leakage among the 300M+ connections served by state-run oil marketing companies **Indian Oil (IOC.NS), BPCL (BPCL.NS), and HPCL (HPCL.NS)**. Tighter verification could cut diversion by an estimated 10–15%, reducing the government’s under-recovery burden and ensuring more reliable subsidy reimbursements for these firms.
- For **IOC.NS**, which alone carried over Rs 10,000 crore in outstanding subsidy receivables in recent quarters, faster, leak-proof transfers would ease working capital strain and improve cash flows, supporting dividend payouts to the government—its majority shareholder. **BPCL.NS** and **HPCL.NS** face similar balance-sheet relief.
- The rule reinforces India’s targeted subsidy delivery, accelerating the shift to clean cooking fuels and potentially dampening kerosene demand.
