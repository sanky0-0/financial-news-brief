Against that backdrop, Kroger (KR) stands out as roughly 9.1% undervalued on GF Value with dividend growth and expansion, while SFDL looks 28.7% overvalued and is exactly the kind of rate-sensitive holding to reassess. Pay attention to GM CFO Paul Jacobson’s warning of a 2027 “flat spot” for EVs as the company pivots toward gas-powered trucks, because it signals near-term margin priorities over electrification. Also watch Anthropic’s Claude gaining traction with small businesses amid AI backlash, suggesting practical AI adoption may be more durable than hype-driven trades. For the UK consumer, NIQ data showing a summer slowdown and a September reset in grocery shopping hints at a back-to-school and holiday demand shift that could move Tesco, Sainsbury, and Ocado. This day matters because higher yields, EV caution, and selective valuations are forcing investors to separate cash-flow compounders from crowded, rate-sensitive names.
