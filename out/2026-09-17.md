# Daily Financial Brief

## Today at a Glance
Today is dominated by two opposing forces: the immediate threat of an oil supply shock and the relentless, often unorthodox funding of AI. Analysts are warning of a “staggering” spike at the pump from any Iran conflict, putting energy equities like the Energy Select Sector SPDR (XLE) and crude futures in sharp focus. At the same time, Apollo Global Management (APO) is reportedly considering a loan from SoftBank to finance its investments in OpenAI, underscoring how private credit is stretching to bridge the AI sector’s massive capital gap. These moves play out against a crucial macro backdrop: the Treasury just detailed its upcoming 2-, 5-, and 7-year note auctions, whose demand will be tested by French deficit pressures and defense spending realities like Northrop Grumman’s (NOC) acceleration of Sentinel ICBM testing. Today matters because it forces investors to simultaneously price a potential energy-driven inflation resurgence and the market’s capacity to absorb fresh sovereign debt while the AI arms race demands ever-more creative capital.
