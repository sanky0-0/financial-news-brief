**Why this matters:**
- Bessent’s push for balanced trade ahead of Trump-Xi talks lowers near-term tariff risk, pulling the 10-year yield (currently ~4.2%) toward 4.15%—a break below that level would signal markets pricing in less inflation, boosting rate-sensitive sectors like homebuilders (XHB, Lennar (LEN)).
- A trade détente raises the odds of a September Fed cut: CME FedWatch now shows a 60% probability, but a constructive Trump-Xi call could push that above 70%, lifting long-duration tech (ARKK) and small-caps (IWM) that rely on lower funding costs.

- The PBoC may allow further yuan weakness (USDCNH above 7.25) to offset any trade shock, widening the US-China rate differential as Chinese 10-year bonds trade below 2.7%—keep an eye on EM currency ETFs (EEM) for contagion if the renminbi slides.
