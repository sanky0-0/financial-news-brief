# Daily Financial Brief

## Today at a Glance
Today’s market revolves around Nvidia (NVDA), which dropped a bombshell: CEO Jensen Huang resigned just as the company reported quarterly earnings that doubled and issued an outlook above expectations. The leadership vacuum at the AI chip titan injects sudden uncertainty into a stock that has been the engine of the tech rally, even as the numbers confirm demand is still exploding. Separately, SpaceX is reportedly plotting a $40 billion financing round to fund AI chip purchases, a sign that the infrastructure race is spreading beyond traditional semiconductor names. Former SEC Chair Jay Clayton also addressed widespread tech outages, a reminder that operational risks lurk beneath the AI boom. Watch Nvidia’s conference call for any succession roadmap—and whether the stock can weather a CEO shock when the earnings themselves are stellar.
