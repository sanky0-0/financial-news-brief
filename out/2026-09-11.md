# Daily Financial Brief

## Today at a Glance
Today’s markets are fixated on the painful combination of $6-a-gallon U.S. gasoline and a 5% handle on the 10-year Treasury yield — numbers that are making the Trump administration wobble. Progress toward reopening the Strait of Hormuz offers a glimmer of relief for crude, but the Iran war is far from over, keeping Texas gas prices elevated and oil volatility high. The ECB’s release of its September 2026 staff macroeconomic projections will give traders a longer-term view on euro-area growth and inflation, potentially shifting rate-cut bets. With energy costs biting consumers and bond markets flashing warning signals, today is a critical juncture to watch inflation expectations and geopolitical developments closely.
