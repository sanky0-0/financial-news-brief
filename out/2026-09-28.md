# Daily Financial Brief

## Today at a Glance
Today’s session pivots on a double shot of de-escalation: the U.S. and China agreed to roll back tariffs on $60 billion of goods, a concrete step toward easing the trade war that could lift multinationals from Apple (AAPL) to Caterpillar (CAT). Simultaneously, Saudi Arabia resumed oil exports via the Red Sea, bypassing the Strait of Hormuz and dealing a strategic blow to Iran just as Tehran floated a plan to reopen the chokepoint. The twin developments threaten to upend crude’s geopolitical risk premium—watch Brent and WTI futures for a potential dip, and keep an eye on energy majors like ExxonMobil (XOM) and shipping stocks. For income investors, the tariff détente may also revive beaten-down China credit and emerging-market debt. This day matters because it tests whether markets can sustain a risk-on rally built on fragile diplomatic breakthroughs.
