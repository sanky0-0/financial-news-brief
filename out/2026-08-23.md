**Why this matters:**
- Alibaba (BABA) seeking >$10B via a share sale to fund AI expansion signals aggressive dilution — a ~6-8% increase in outstanding shares could pressure BABA stock near-term, especially if priced at a discount to current levels.
- The capital raise intensifies China’s AI cloud battle: BABA’s Cloud division directly competes with Tencent (0700.HK) and Baidu (BIDU) — a $10B war chest may force rivals to accelerate their own AI investments or risk losing enterprise market share in areas like large language models and AI training services.

**Why this matters:**

- AMD’s parallel advances in x86 server CPUs (gaining over 30% unit share vs. Intel) combine with AI GPU gains to position AMD as a more balanced, lower-risk play on data center spend — while NVDA’s singular AI momentum makes it vulnerable to any pullback in AI capex narratives.
