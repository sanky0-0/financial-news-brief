**Continental Resources to develop massive oil patch in Venezuela** — [→ cnbc.com](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/16/continental-resources-to-develop-massive-oil-patch-in-venezuela-.html)

**Why this matters:**
- National Fuel Gas (NFG) exploring options for its $5B natural gas production arm signals a potential exit from upstream to focus on its regulated utility and pipeline segments — a move that could attract E&P consolidators like EQT (EQT) or Expand Energy (EXE) and set a valuation benchmark for Appalachian gas peers such as CNX Resources (CNX) and Antero Resources (AR).
- Continental Resources’ (private, formerly CLR) bold entry into Venezuela’s massive oil patch, despite sanctions risk, underscores a high-stakes hunt for growth outside U.S. shale — this could pressure publicly traded international operators like Chevron (CVX) and Hess (HES), which already hold Venezuelan exposure, and may test the boundaries of U.S. foreign policy for other independents.
- The strategic divergence — NFG monetizing domestic gas assets while Continental chases oil in geopolitically fraught Venezuela — highlights a sector split: U.S. natural gas producers are retrenching amid depressed Henry Hub prices, while well-capitalized oil-focused players seek underexploited reserves, potentially reshaping competitive dynamics and M&A appetites across the energy complex.
