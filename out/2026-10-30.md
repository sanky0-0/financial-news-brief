# Daily Financial Brief

## Today at a Glance
Today’s most compelling move comes from the Treasury, which just hiked the I Bond fixed rate to 1.40%—the highest since 2006—pushing the annualized combined rate to 5.51% for purchases through April. That’s a rare, risk-free yield worth acting on before the May reset. Meanwhile, MasTec (MTZ) revealed it expects to convert over $1 billion of gas-generation projects into its record $24.5 billion backlog, a concrete signal that the physical buildout to power data centers and electrification is accelerating regardless of AI sentiment. Pay attention to that divergence: while capital pours into steel and turbines, the question “What happens when the AI boom ends?” hangs over the very demand forecasts driving that spending. Today matters because it offers both a guaranteed 5.51% anchor for your cash and a reminder that the real-economy infrastructure boom may outlast the hype cycle—but not without risk if the AI trade stumbles.
