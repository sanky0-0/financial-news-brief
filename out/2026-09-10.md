### AI Products & Launches
**OpenAI Cuts Off Rivals’ Ads on ChatGPT** — [→ pymnts.com](https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/openai-cuts-off-rivals-ads-chatgpt/)

**Why this matters:**
- OpenAI is weaponizing ChatGPT’s distribution: it will sell ad slots to non-AI brands but excludes Alphabet’s Gemini (GOOGL), Microsoft Copilot (MSFT), Anthropic (backed by AMZN), and xAI’s Grok, protecting its own assistant on a reported 800M+ weekly-user surface.
- This makes OpenAI a direct ad platform rival to Google (GOOGL) and Meta (META); as ChatGPT ad inventory scales, AI-related ad pricing and brand budgets could shift away from search/social, creating a new performance-ad channel.
- Microsoft (MSFT) faces sharper channel conflict: its ~$13B OpenAI stake now coexists with a blocked Copilot ad campaign, which could strain Azure/compute negotiations and accelerate Microsoft’s competing Copilot/Bing ad push.
- Antitrust risk rises: excluding AI rivals from a leading assistant’s ad marketplace looks like self-preferencing, which could invite FTC/DOJ scrutiny and potentially force ad-access remedies or ad-load limits.
