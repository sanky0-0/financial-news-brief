**Nvidia bulls may have a small problem on their hands** — [→ finance.yahoo.com](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/article/nvidia-bulls-may-have-a-small-problem-on-their-hands-142341243.html)

**Why this matters:**
- Nvidia’s (NVDA) next-gen Blackwell GPUs rely on TSMC’s 3nm process and advanced CoWoS packaging — the same manufacturing complexity that drives demand for KLA’s (KLAC) inspection and metrology tools, as smaller nodes and 3D structures require more defect detection steps.
- If Nvidia’s “small problem” involves yield bottlenecks or supply constraints on these complex chips, KLA directly benefits: fabs and packaging partners will need to accelerate process control investments to ramp output, boosting KLAC’s orders.
- The dynamic creates a hedge: even if Nvidia’s growth temporarily slows due to manufacturing challenges, KLA captures spending from the broader semiconductor ecosystem trying to solve those exact complexity issues, insulating KLAC from single-customer demand swings.
