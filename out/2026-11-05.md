**Power grid costs to be borne by businesses, US OpenAI policy proposal** — [→ this.kiji.is](https://news.jp/i/1491171593145074429)

**Why this matters:**
- OpenAI’s push to make businesses bear power grid upgrade costs directly targets hyperscalers like Microsoft (MSFT), Amazon (AMZN), and Alphabet (GOOGL) — their data center expansion plans could face billions in additional capex, squeezing cloud margins.
- Utilities such as Dominion Energy (D) and Duke Energy (DUK) may avoid rate hikes for residential customers, but if tech firms delay or scale back projects due to higher costs, utility growth forecasts tied to data center demand (e.g., Dominion’s 4.6 GW contracted pipeline) could be revised down.
- This policy shift could accelerate the divergence between AI-first cloud providers and traditional enterprises; watch for potential re-rating of power-exposed REITs like Digital Realty (DLR) and Equinix (EQIX) if grid access becomes a competitive moat.
