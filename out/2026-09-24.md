Watch post-buyback earnings guidance for a read on corporate confidence, and whether the bond substitute trade can withstand further rate volatility.

### Geopolitics
**Senate to vote on ending Iran war as Americans face higher fuel costs from conflict** — [→ cnbc.com](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/24/iran-war-powers-senate-vote-fuel.html)

**Why this matters:**
- Brent crude (LCOc1) has jumped ~10% in the past month to above $85/barrel on Iran supply disruption fears, lifting energy stocks like ExxonMobil (XOM) and the Energy Select Sector SPDR (XLE) — a Senate vote to de-escalate could trigger a sharp reversal in oil prices and wipe out recent gains in the sector.  
- US refiners such as Valero (VLO) and Marathon Petroleum (MPC) are passing through higher crude costs to consumers, with average US gasoline prices up $0.30 to $3.80/gallon — a resolution would ease input costs and likely compress refining margins, hitting these stocks.  
- Airlines and logistics firms, including Delta (DAL) and FedEx (FDX), have warned of fuel cost headwinds in current-quarter guidance; a de-escalation vote that reduces jet fuel prices could provide immediate earnings relief and boost share prices.
