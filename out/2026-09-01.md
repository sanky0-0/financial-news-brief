# Daily Financial Brief

## Today at a Glance
Treasury Secretary Bessent’s sit-down with BOJ Governor Ueda dominated macro, as his call for “sound policy” to avert currency volatility and his simultaneous praise for bond-market discipline sent the 10-year yield spiking and battered gold (XAU) to a two-week low. With market-implied rate-hike odds near 70%, non-yielding assets are under fire—bullion testing a strategist’s key level while the yen wobbles, making FX and rates the day’s must-watch tape. For equity players, the turnaround story belongs to Sonos (SONO), which is attempting to shake off its 2024 app disaster by unveiling three new products and an AI audio OS; a bold, speculative reopening trade that hinges on execution. Meanwhile, BugBase’s launch of an AI Pentest Copilot for enterprises underscores the quiet ascent of cybersecurity-as-a-service. Going forward, the entwined path of U.S. yields, the dollar-yen pair, and Bessent’s next remarks will set the risk appetite for everything from commodities to tech, with geopolitics from Kyiv’s deadly strike adding an anxious undertow that hasn’t yet triggered a flight to safety.
