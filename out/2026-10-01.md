# Daily Financial Brief

## Today at a Glance
Fed Vice Chair Philip Jefferson noted that pressure in the Treasury market reflects a genuine reassessment of the economic outlook by investors, a remark that could signal a more cautious rate trajectory ahead. Singapore’s Temasek, meanwhile, expressed comfort with current AI spending levels, offering a steadying vote of confidence for tech investors wary of capex bloat. On the geopolitical front, Iran’s conditional offer to allow nuclear inspectors in exchange for sanctions relief introduces a potential de-escalation that might ease oil supply fears. In equities, Constellium (CSTM) faces mounting margin pressure as the spread between feedstock costs and end-product prices keeps widening—a dynamic worth monitoring. Watch for further Fed commentary and any concrete progress on Iran talks, both of which could swiftly shift rate and commodity expectations.
