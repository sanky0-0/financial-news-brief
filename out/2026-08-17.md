**Why this matters:**
- HIVE Digital (HIVE) surged 14% after revealing an NVIDIA (NVDA) partnership for its AI cloud expansion, underscoring NVIDIA’s push to diversify GPU customers beyond hyperscalers to converted bitcoin miners like HIVE — watch for similar deals with Core Scientific (CORZ) or Hut 8 (HUT).
