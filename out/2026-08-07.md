**Why this matters:**
- Nvidia (NVDA) briefly eclipsed Apple (AAPL) in market cap on June 5, with both hovering near $3 trillion — the neck-and-neck race signals that AI chip demand (NVDA data center revenue +427% YoY) now rivals the value of Apple’s entire consumer ecosystem.
- The showdown sharpens a divide in the chip sector: Apple’s custom silicon (M2/M3) for on-device AI in iOS 18 accelerates demand for TSMC’s (TSM) 3nm capacity, while Nvidia’s H100/H200 GPUs are the backbone of cloud AI training — TSMC is the common bottleneck and critical supplier to both.
- Apple’s rumored push into proprietary AI server chips could reduce its reliance on Nvidia’s data center GPUs for model training; if scaled, this may pressure NVDA’s forward multiple and embolden other hyperscalers to design custom silicon, reshaping AI chip demand.
- For investors, the battle highlights two semiconductor plays: Nvidia’s pure-play AI exposure with high growth but concentration risk, versus Apple’s integrated hardware-software model with stable cash flows — the tiebreaker may be TSMC, which booked an 8% monthly revenue jump in May on strong AI and smartphone orders from both.
