**Why this matters:**
- SEBI’s traffic-light rating system could compress the information advantage of credit rating agencies like CRISIL (CRISIL.NS), ICRA (ICRA.NS), and CARE Ratings (CARE.NS) — watch for potential fee pressure if simplified ratings reduce demand for detailed credit reports, especially in the ₹40 lakh crore Indian corporate bond market.
- A consumer-friendly color code (green/yellow/red) may accelerate retail inflows into lower-rated AA and A bonds, narrowing spreads for NBFCs like Bajaj Finance (BAJFINANCE.NS) and Shriram Finance (SHRIAMFIN.NS) that rely on bond markets for 30-40% of their borrowing.
