**ET Has Opportunities With Its Gas Business But Risks With Its Oil Business. (NYSE:ET)** — [→ seekingalpha.com](https://seekingalpha.com/article/4941022-et-has-opportunities-with-its-gas-business-but-risks-with-its-oil-business)

**Why this matters:**
- Japan’s proposed easing of replacement rules for thermal, geothermal, and wind facilities accelerates the phase-out of fossil fuel generation in the world’s second-largest LNG importer — directly threatening long-term demand for US LNG exports and the gas infrastructure growth that Energy Transfer (ET) is banking on.
- ET’s gas business opportunities (e.g., Gulf Coast LNG terminals) face a structural demand risk if Asian buyers like Japan cut thermal power reliance, while ET’s oil segment risks are compounded by potential crude demand erosion in key markets.
- The regulatory shift could speed retirement of gas-fired plants in Japan, pressuring global LNG prices and volumes — a negative for ET’s natural gas pipeline and export margins, even as near-term gas demand stays strong.
